# A practical checklist before you publish anything online

Before you hit publish, run this short list. It catches the mistakes that make readers bounce — vague titles, missing context, and walls of text.

## Clarity

- Can a stranger understand the promise from the title alone?
- Does the first paragraph say who this is for and what they will get?
- Did you cut filler openers (“In today’s world…”)?

## Trust

- Are claims specific enough to verify?
- Did you link to sources where you rely on data?
- Is the tone helpful instead of hype?

## Finish strong

- End with one clear next step the reader can take today.
