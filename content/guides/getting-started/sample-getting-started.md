# How to start learning a new skill without burning out

Most people quit in week two because they aim for intensity, not rhythm. Start with three short sessions each week, keep them under twenty minutes, and only increase difficulty when the last session felt easy.

## A simple weekly plan

Day one: review the basics and practice one tiny drill. Day two: rest or light reading. Day three: practice again and write one sentence about what improved. Consistency beats heroic weekends.

> Show up more often than you go hard — especially in the first month.

## Track progress, not perfection

Keep a short log: date, minutes, and one note. If you miss a day, restart the next one without a guilt spiral. The goal is a streak of attempts, not a perfect calendar.
